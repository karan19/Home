Kalam and Einstein: artwork & recordings

In the house, click the downstairs bookshelf for Kalam or the upstairs digital clock for Einstein. Click again to stop. Their portraits form on the walls while the recordings play.

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Portrait: “Dr.APJ.Abdul Kalam” by athithya, published on Openclipart, with a public-domain copy on FreeSVG. The original SVG illustration was retraced into monochrome SVG paths for this house.

Audio: APJ Abdul Kalam speech, recorded by Fotokannan, described by the source as Trivandrum 2014. CC BY-SA 4.0. Audio extracted from the full 57-second video, converted to mono MP3 and loudness normalized. Adapted audio remains CC BY-SA 4.0.

Albert Einstein

Portrait: Albert Einstein.svg by Manco Capac (2008), optimized by Smasongarrison (2022). CC BY-SA 3.0. Retraced into charcoal SVG paths, centered and resized; the adapted SVG remains CC BY-SA 3.0.
