House portraits: artwork & recordings

In the house, click the upstairs digital clock for Einstein, or the chess set upstairs for Magnus Carlsen. Click again to stop. Their portraits form on the walls while the recordings play.

Magnus Carlsen

In the house, click the chess set on the small table upstairs. One of four clips plays at random on each click while his portrait forms on the back wall and mirrors on the roof. Each file is named by the opening words of the clip. Portrait: line-art illustration created with the built-in image-generation tool, traced into monochrome SVG paths. Audio: Carlsen's own voice, cut from four Instagram reels — an interview excerpt shared by @jtgstoic (“If you want to be great at something, you have to be obsessed with it”), an interview excerpt shared by @ChessIconic (his 10,000-hours answer), his opening line from the Netflix documentary Untold: Chess Mates, in a reel shared by Judd mcCall, and an interview excerpt shared by Zachary Saine on what separates good players from great ones. Short excerpts used for a personal fan project. No endorsement is implied.

Steve Jobs

In the house, click the pillow on the upstairs bed. One of six Jobs clips plays at random on each click while his portrait forms on the wall. Each file is named by the opening words of the clip. Speech excerpts were cut from these recordings: “Your time is limited…” — Stanford commencement address, 2005; “I'm convinced that about half…” — Smithsonian oral history, April 1995; “You've got to start with the customer experience…” — WWDC 1997 Q&A; “I don't really care about being right…” and “In my career I found that the best people…” — The Lost Interview, 1995; “Life can be much broader…” — Santa Clara Valley Historical Association interview, 1994.

Dwight Schrute

In the house, click the backyard lamp post on the kitchen side (back-left corner of the yard). One of five Dwight Schrute clips plays at random on each click while his portrait forms on the back wall above the kitchen and mirrors on the roof. Each file is named by the signature line of the clip. Portrait: stencil illustration supplied by the site owner, traced into monochrome SVG paths. Audio: dialogue excerpts from The Office (NBC) — “Don't be an idiot…”, the volunteer sheriff's deputy, the weaponry, and the loyalty clips were cut from the official The Office YouTube compilation “DWIGHT'S BEST QUOTES”; “There's no limit to what I think I deserve” is from Season 8, Episode 19, “Get the Girl”. Each clip carries a dramatic music bed mixed about 7 dB below the voice (the same treatment as the Steve Jobs clips): 'Titan' and 'Ascension' by Scott Buckley — released under CC-BY 4.0. www.scottbuckley.com.au; 'Clash Defiant' and 'Five Armies' by Kevin MacLeod — released under CC-BY 4.0. incompetech.com. Music license: CC BY 4.0. Dwight Schrute is a fictional character; clips are short excerpts used for a personal fan project. No endorsement is implied.
